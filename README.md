# Rail-Vision: AI-Powered Railway Traffic Optimization System

## Overview

Rail-Vision is an intelligent railway traffic management and optimization system designed to maximize railway section throughput, reduce congestion, and improve train scheduling efficiency.

The system uses optimization techniques, railway operational constraints, and explainable AI (XAI) to generate efficient train movement plans while ensuring safe and conflict-free operations.

---

## Problem Statement

Railway networks often experience congestion due to:

* Limited track capacity
* Scheduling conflicts
* Inefficient train slot allocation
* Delays caused by manual planning

Rail-Vision addresses these challenges by automatically optimizing train movements and generating actionable recommendations.

---

## Key Features

### Train Scheduling Optimization

* Allocates train slots efficiently
* Minimizes scheduling conflicts
* Improves section utilization

### Throughput Maximization

* Maximizes the number of trains passing through a section
* Optimizes resource usage

### Constraint-Based Optimization

* Implements railway operational constraints
* Ensures safe train movement

###  Explainable AI (XAI)

* Provides reasons behind optimization decisions
* Improves transparency and trust

###  ATC Signal Generation

* Generates operational recommendations
* Supports automated traffic control concepts

###  KPI Monitoring

Tracks important performance indicators such as:

* Total trains scheduled
* Throughput achieved
* Resource utilization
* Delay reduction metrics

###  Distance-Time Graph Visualization

* Visualizes train movements
* Helps identify bottlenecks
* Supports traffic analysis
